How to play

← →Swim left and right
Space / ↑Jump
DShoot a bubble
FShoot a poison bubble

Bubbles trap puffer fish and jelly fish. A fin slap knocks out puffer fish. Normal bubbles hurt the whale a little, poison bubbles hurt him a lot, so collect the poison bottles.
